## Biography

The Apich Organization is an open-source organization dedicated in advancing the Rust ecosystem, with a primary focus on scientific computation. Our mission is to foster a more secure and peaceful cyberspace and contributing to the Rust ecosystem. Our work is driven by three pillars: our Development and Security teams, supported by a specialized Consultancy group. We actively welcome community contributions and value the insights brought by outside collaborators and contributors. For inquiries or collaboration opportunities, feel free to reach out to us at **info@apich.org**.
